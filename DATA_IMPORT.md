# Importing products, prices and opening stock

We use Frappe's own **Data Import** tool. We build nothing for this. The three sample files in
[`import-templates/`](import-templates/) have the exact column names Frappe expects (generated by
Frappe's exporter) and were tested on the dev site.

| Order | File | Creates | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `1-products.csv` | Products with barcode, extra units (Box = 12) and batch/expiry settings | One product can span several rows: extra barcodes or units go on the following rows with the first columns left empty |
| 2 | `2-prices.csv` | Channel prices per product and unit | Price list must be `Wholesale`, `Online` or `Self-delivery`. A unit price needs the unit to exist on the product (step 1). |
| 3 | `3-opening-stock.csv` | An **Opening Stock** reconciliation (draft) | Review it in Desk, then submit. It posts the stock value against "Temporary Opening - AT". |

## How to run it

1. In Desk, open **Data Import** → New. Choose the document type (Item, Item Price, Stock Reconciliation) and **Insert New Records**.
2. Attach the CSV. Frappe shows a preview and lists any problem rows. Fix them and start the import.
3. Do the files in order (1, 2, 3).

## Limits (honest)

- Opening stock for **batch / expiry products** is not covered by the reconciliation file. Receive those with a Purchase Receipt or Stock Entry that carries the batch and expiry date. This was not tested.
- Replace the sample rows (`IMP-…`) with real data. Do not import the samples into a real site.
- Barcodes must be valid. For type `EAN` Frappe checks the check digit.
